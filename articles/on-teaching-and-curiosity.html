On teaching and curiosity

May 2026 · Essay

The best classes I have taught were never the ones where I sounded the most certain. They were the ones where a student asked a question I had not rehearsed, and the room paused long enough for all of us to think together.

Curiosity, I think, is not a personality trait reserved for the young. It is a habit — one that survives only when people feel safe enough to not know things out loud.

A lecture can deliver information. A conversation can change how someone sees.
