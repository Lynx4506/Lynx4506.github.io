Two cats, one apartment

April 2026 · Notes

There is a particular hour in the evening when both cats decide the apartment belongs to them alone. One watches the street from the window. The other inspects the kitchen with the seriousness of a health inspector.

Living with them has taught me that attention is not always loud. Sometimes it looks like a tail flick, a slow blink, or the quiet decision to sit beside you while you grade papers.
